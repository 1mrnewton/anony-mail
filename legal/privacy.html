Anony Mail

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Anony Mail is anonymous by design. There are no user accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number. This policy explains what the hosted service at anonymail.store, operated by Scyther Alpha, processes and why. If you point the app at a self-hosted server, your data goes to that server’s operator instead, and this policy does not apply.

What we process

  • Received email. Messages sent to your disposable addresses (content, attachments, and headers) are stored so the app can show them to you. They are permanently deleted when you delete them or when the mailbox expires, whichever comes first.
  • Mailbox secrets. Each mailbox is controlled by a random key generated for your device. We store only what is needed to authenticate requests — nothing that identifies you personally.
  • Push tokens. If you enable notifications, we store your device’s push token to deliver new-mail alerts through Apple. It is removed when you disable notifications or the mailbox expires.
  • Purchase state. Pro purchases are processed by Apple and validated through RevenueCat. We see an anonymous identifier and whether the Pro entitlement is active — never your payment details or Apple Account identity.
  • Server logs. IP addresses appear briefly in operational logs for rate limiting and abuse prevention, and are kept only for a short period.

What we don’t do

  • No selling or sharing of data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No ads and no cross-app tracking.
  • No profiling of message content — mail is processed only to deliver and display it to you.

Third parties

We rely on Apple (payments and push notifications) and RevenueCat (subscription management). Each processes data under its own privacy policy. Email is, by nature, sent to us by third-party mail servers over the public internet.

Retention

Mailboxes and their messages are deleted automatically at expiry. Deleting a mailbox in the app removes it and its messages immediately. Backups and logs age out on short, fixed schedules.

Your choices

You can delete any mailbox or message at any time in the app, disable push notifications, or stop using the Service — nothing about you remains once your mailboxes expire. For anything else, contact us.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above reflects the current version.

Anony Mail

Support

Anony Mail gives you disposable, anonymous email addresses that expire automatically. No account, no sign-up — mailbox keys live only on your device.

Contact

Email us at support@anonymail.store and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include the app version (Settings → About) and a description of the problem.

Common questions

  • Where did my mailbox go? Mailboxes expire and are permanently deleted at the end of their lifetime. Expired mailboxes and their messages cannot be recovered.
  • Subscriptions and refunds. Pro is billed through Apple. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings, and use “Restore purchases” in the app's settings on a new device. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Self-hosting. The server is open source — Pro users can point the app at their own instance from Settings → Server.
